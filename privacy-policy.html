Your loan data

EMI Freedom Tracker does not require an account or connect to your bank. Loan details, account nicknames, payment confirmations and prepayments are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not transmit these loan records to the developer, Google Ads or Google Play.

Backups and deletion

You can export a readable JSON backup or CSV schedule to a location you choose. Protect these files as financial information. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Delete loans in the app or clear its storage in Android Settings to erase local records. Uninstalling removes local app storage. Copies you exported must be deleted separately.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display a banner. Google’s advertising services may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising interactions, app interactions, diagnostic information and other data described in Google’s policies. The app requests consent information through Google’s User Messaging Platform before initializing and requesting ads. Where applicable, you can revisit ad privacy choices in More. Ads require an internet connection.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Support messages and retention

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, message and any information you choose to include to respond and resolve your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Local loan records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete those copies. Google's retention and processing of advertising and purchase data are governed by its linked policies.

Optional support purchases

Optional coffee support purchases use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details; the app does not receive card or bank credentials. The app receives product and purchase status information to complete and acknowledge the transaction. A purchase does not unlock app features.

Permissions and reminders

If you grant notification permission and enable reminders, the app periodically checks local loans for upcoming or overdue payments. Android may delay reminders. The app uses internet access for ads, consent and Play purchases, and Android’s document picker for files you explicitly choose.

Children

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and above managing their own loans and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through a support message, contact us using the email address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app behavior or service providers change. Contact the developer at the address above with privacy questions.

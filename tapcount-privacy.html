Privacy Policy

App: TapCount (Android package: app.tapcount)
Developer: satish17s
Contact: satishsharma.cse@gmail.com
Effective date: October 28, 2026

TapCount helps you understand your phone habits. Your phone usage history is processed and saved on your device. TapCount does not upload that history to the developer or include it in advertising requests. Google services separately handle advertising, consent, and optional purchases as explained below.

Phone activity and Usage access

After you grant Android Usage access, TapCount reads app foreground events, app package identifiers, event times, and lock-screen or screen-off events. It uses these events to estimate interactions and count app switches and unlocks. It can also look up app labels and launcher apps on your device to display your activity and filter system activity. TapCount does not count individual screen taps or read the contents of other apps.

Daily totals, hourly totals, per-app counts and package identifiers are saved in the app's private storage for comparisons, trends, records, and recaps. Android may allow TapCount to import recent activity from before you granted access. Periodic background work updates the local archive while Usage access remains enabled. No TapCount account is required, and there is no developer-operated activity server or separate analytics service.

Advertising and consent

The free version includes Google AdMob banner ads. Depending on consent, device settings, and service availability, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising and other device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Ad-related network traffic is encrypted in transit.

TapCount uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent information and present consent forms where required. Where available, use Settings > Ad privacy choices to review your choices. Android settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Pro removes banner ads and banner requests; Google SDK initialization or consent checks may still occur. Internal test versions may show Google test ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Optional Pro purchases

Where enabled, lifetime Pro uses Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details and maintains purchase records. TapCount receives product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and signed purchase information to verify, acknowledge, and restore access. The app stores a local Pro entitlement flag. It does not receive your full payment card details or banking credentials, and does not upload purchase information to a developer-operated server. Purchases may be unavailable in setup or internal test builds.

Sharing is your choice

You can choose to share a daily app-switch count as text or a monthly recap as an image through Android's share sheet. These summaries include aggregate activity counts and the relevant day or month, without app names. The receiving app or service processes shared content under its own privacy policy. Demo summaries are labeled as sample data.

Monthly images are generated in a private cache folder. Generating another recap removes previous recap images from that folder. Copies you send or save elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Retention and deletion

  • On an archive refresh, TapCount removes daily records older than approximately two years (730 days). Records may remain on your device until another refresh or until you delete them.
  • Settings > Delete saved history deletes TapCount's local daily archive. It keeps your Pro entitlement and other settings. It does not delete Android's own usage records, which may be imported again on a later refresh.
  • Revoke Usage access in Android Settings to stop TapCount from reading further phone activity. Revoking access does not itself erase previously saved records.
  • Clearing TapCount's app storage or uninstalling removes its local archive, settings, and cached images. A Pro purchase remains with Google Play and can be restored using the same Google Play account.

Google retains advertising, consent, and purchase data according to its policies and applicable settings. Deleting TapCount's local history does not delete records held by Google or copies you shared with other services.

Permissions and security

TapCount uses Usage access for counting, internet and network-state access for Google services, and advertising-ID permissions used by the advertising SDK. It does not request contacts, precise device location, camera, microphone, notification content, screen recording, or Accessibility access. It does not read messages, passwords, or the content displayed inside other apps.

Activity records use Android's private app storage. The app disables automatic Android cloud backup and excludes its data from Android device transfer. The app does not sell your phone activity history or provide it to advertisers. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Avoid including sensitive activity or payment information unless it is necessary for your request.
